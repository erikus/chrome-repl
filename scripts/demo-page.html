Banana Bread

Makes 1 loaf (8 x 4 in pan) · 12 slices · 15 min prep · 60 min bake at 350°F

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas, mashed (about 1 1/4 cups)
  • 1/3 cup butter, melted
  • 3/4 cup sugar
  • 1 egg, beaten
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1 tsp baking soda and a pinch of salt
  • 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 350°F and butter the pan.
  2. Stir the butter into the bananas, then the sugar, egg and vanilla.
  3. Sprinkle over the baking soda and salt; fold in the flour.
  4. Bake 55 to 65 minutes, until a skewer comes out clean.
